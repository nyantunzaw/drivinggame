How to play

Touch

Left side: move / steer (push up to accelerate, down to reverse).

Right side: drag to look. GAS, BRAKE, DRIFT (handbrake).

CAR: get in the nearest car (yes, even if someone's driving it).

Keyboard

WASD / arrows move & drive · Space jump / handbrake · Shift sprint

F enter/exit · E punch · H horn · R radio · C camera · T taxi job · M map · Esc pause

Gamepads work too.

Yangon tips

Pink M markers start missions. Cyan $ shops repaint your car and lose the cops for $100. Find all 20 hidden Golden Hintha statuettes. You can't swim — stay out of Kandawgyi and Inya lakes! Traffic drives on the right, but most cars are right-hand drive, so you climb out on the right.

Credits

A fan-made homage to 80s open-world crime capers, set on a real map of Yangon, Myanmar. Not affiliated with any game publisher.

Map data: . Roads, buildings, lakes, parks and township boundaries are real; heights of most buildings are estimated.

3D engine: three.js (MIT). All models, textures, music and sound are generated in code.
